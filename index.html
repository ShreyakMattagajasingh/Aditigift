Trainer guide // Minigames

Game rulebook

Choose a game below. Connect Four and Mancala require both players in the same online room. The claw machine is played separately.

Connect Four // Kung Fu Tea

Start

Both players enter Kung Fu Tea in the same multiplayer room. Press E at the board. The first player waits until the second player approaches the table and joins.

Controls

Left / RightSelect a column E / SpaceDrop a piece MouseChoose and play a column EscLeave the board RRematch after a result

Rules

  • Aditi uses red pieces. Shreyak uses yellow pieces.
  • Players alternate dropping one piece into a non-full column.
  • The first horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of four wins.
  • A completely full board without four connected pieces is a draw.
  • The player who did not start the previous match starts the rematch.
  • If someone leaves, the board resets and the remaining player waits.
Mancala // Sencha Tea Bar

Start

Both players enter Sencha in the same multiplayer room. Press E at the Mancala table. The first player waits for the second player to join.

Controls

Left / RightSelect one of your six pits E / SpaceSow the selected beads MouseChoose and sow a pit EscLeave the table RRematch after a result

Rules

  1. Each of the twelve small pits begins with four beads.
  2. Choose a non-empty pit on your side and sow its beads one at a time.
  3. Place a bead in your own store, but skip the opponent's store.
  4. If the final bead lands in your store, take another turn.
  5. If it lands in an empty pit on your side, capture it and all beads in the opposite pit.
  6. When one side is empty, the other player collects all remaining beads.
  7. The largest store total wins. Equal totals produce a draw.
Clawcade // Chocolate Shoppe

Start

Press E at the claw machine. After its opening animation, you have seven seconds to position the claw.

Controls

A / DSteer left or right ArrowsSteer left or right SpaceDrop the claw or play again EscLeave the machine

Rules

  • The claw accelerates and carries momentum while moving.
  • Time running out drops the claw automatically.
  • Align the claw closely with a plush to catch it.
  • Every caught plush has a 40 percent chance to slip.
  • A secured plush travels to the bottom-left chute and enters the active character's inventory.
  • Open the inventory with Q after leaving the machine.
