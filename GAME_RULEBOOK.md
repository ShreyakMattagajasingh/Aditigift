# Aditi's World Game Rulebook

Madison Edition - current game rules

## Quick Controls

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Interact with a game | `E` |
| Close or leave a game | `Esc` |
| Open inventory | `Q` |
| Move through the world | `WASD` or arrow keys |

Connect Four and Mancala require both players to be connected to the same
multiplayer room. The claw machine is a solo activity and can be played by
either character independently.

## Connect Four

**Location:** Kung Fu Tea  
**Players:** Aditi and Shreyak  
**Type:** Synchronized two-player strategy game

### Starting a Match

1. Both players must host or join the same multiplayer room.
2. Both players must enter Kung Fu Tea and walk near the Connect Four table.
3. The first player presses `E` at the board.
4. The game displays `WAITING FOR PLAYER 2`.
5. When the second player reaches the table and joins, the match begins.
6. The player who joined the table first takes the first turn.

If a waiting invitation is active, the second player's board opens
automatically when they stand near the Connect Four table.

### Player Colors

| Player | Piece |
| --- | --- |
| Aditi | Red |
| Shreyak | Yellow |

### Controls

| Action | Key or Input |
| --- | --- |
| Select a column | Left and Right arrows |
| Select a column with the mouse | Point at a column |
| Drop a piece | `E`, `Space`, or click a column |
| Leave the match | `Esc` or the `X` button |
| Start a rematch | `R` or the Rematch button |

### Rules

- The board has seven columns and six rows.
- Players alternate turns.
- On a turn, choose any column that is not full.
- The piece falls into the lowest empty space in that column.
- A move cannot be made while it is the other player's turn.
- A piece cannot be placed in a full column.

### Winning

The first player to connect four of their pieces wins. The four pieces may be:

- Horizontal
- Vertical
- Diagonal

If every space is filled and neither player has four connected pieces, the
match is a draw.

### Leaving and Rematches

- The Rematch control appears after a win or draw.
- The player who did not start the previous match starts the rematch.
- If either player leaves during a match, the board resets.
- The remaining player returns to the waiting state until the other player
  joins again.

## Mancala

**Location:** Sencha Tea Bar  
**Players:** Aditi and Shreyak  
**Type:** Synchronized two-player counting and capture game

### Starting a Match

1. Both players must be connected to the same multiplayer room.
2. Both players enter Sencha and stand near the Mancala table.
3. The first player presses `E` at the table.
4. The board waits for the second player.
5. The match starts when the second player joins at the table.
6. The player who joined the table first takes the first turn.

If one player is already waiting, the game opens for the second player when
they approach the Mancala table.

### Board Setup

- Each player has six small pits.
- Every small pit begins with four beads.
- There are 48 beads in total.
- Each player has one large scoring store.
- Aditi's pits are on the bottom row and her store is on the right.
- Shreyak's pits are on the top row and his store is on the left.

### Controls

| Action | Key or Input |
| --- | --- |
| Select one of your pits | Left and Right arrows |
| Select a pit with the mouse | Point at one of your pits |
| Sow the selected beads | `E`, `Space`, or click the pit |
| Leave the match | `Esc` or the `X` button |
| Start a rematch | `R` or the Rematch button |

### Taking a Turn

1. Select one non-empty pit on your side.
2. All beads are removed from that pit.
3. The beads are placed one at a time into the following pits.
4. Your own scoring store receives a bead when the sowing path reaches it.
5. The opponent's scoring store is skipped.

Aditi sows across the bottom row toward her store on the right. Shreyak sows
across the top row toward his store on the left.

### Extra Turns

If the final bead lands in your own scoring store, you immediately take
another turn.

### Captures

If the final bead lands in an empty pit on your own side and the opposite pit
contains beads:

1. The final bead is captured.
2. Every bead in the opposite pit is captured.
3. All captured beads move into your scoring store.

### Ending the Match

The match ends when all six pits on either player's side are empty.

- Any beads remaining on the other side move into that player's store.
- The player with the most beads in their store wins.
- Equal store totals produce a draw.

The game always preserves all 48 beads between the pits and stores.

### Leaving and Rematches

- The final score is displayed after the board is cleared.
- The Rematch control appears after a win or draw.
- The other player starts the next match.
- Leaving an unfinished match resets the Mancala board.
- The remaining player waits for the other player to return.

## Clawcade: Sweet Scoop

**Location:** Chocolate Shoppe  
**Players:** One player per attempt  
**Type:** Timed prize game

### Starting a Round

1. Walk to the claw machine inside Chocolate Shoppe.
2. Press `E`.
3. The claw rises from the prize chute.
4. It slides into its starting position.
5. The seven-second timer begins when control is given to the player.

### Controls

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Move the claw left | `A` or Left arrow |
| Move the claw right | `D` or Right arrow |
| Drop the claw | `Space` |
| Play another round | `Space` after the result |
| Leave the machine | `Esc` |

The claw has acceleration and momentum. Releasing a movement key slows it
gradually rather than stopping it instantly.

### Catching a Prize

- Position the claw directly over a plush.
- Press `Space` to drop it.
- If time reaches zero, the claw drops automatically.
- A claw that is too far from every plush returns empty.
- A correctly aligned claw picks up the nearest plush.

### Slip Chance

Every caught plush has a 40 percent chance to slip before reaching the prize
chute. A slipped plush returns to the machine and the attempt is recorded as a
miss.

### Winning a Prize

When a plush remains in the claw:

1. The claw carries it to the bottom-left prize chute.
2. The plush drops into the chute.
3. The named plush is added to the active character's inventory.
4. Press `Q` after leaving the machine to see the prize.

Each successful round awards one plush. Either Aditi or Shreyak can keep a
prize in their own inventory.

## Activities That Are Not Competitive Games

The photo frames, photo booth, music player, inventory gifting, bus stop, and
room interactions are world activities. They do not currently have scores,
turns, winners, or match rules, so they are not included as games in this
edition of the rulebook.
